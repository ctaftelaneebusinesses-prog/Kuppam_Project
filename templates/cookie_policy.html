Browser storage

Your browser's local storage keeps a few preferences on your device only: your chosen light or dark theme, the location or city you selected, and whether you dismissed the notification prompt. These are not sent to us on each request.

Third-party services

  • Google Fonts serves the site's fonts, so Google receives your IP address when a page loads.
  • Google Maps is embedded on the Contact page and may set its own cookies when it loads.
  • Google Sign-In and Supabase handle authentication when you sign in.
  • Links to WhatsApp open WhatsApp only when you click them.

These services have their own privacy policies and cookie practices.

Managing cookies

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. If you block the essential cookies, you will not be able to sign in or submit forms.
