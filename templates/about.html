Who Are We?

Hello Kuppam is a comprehensive local information platform designed to connect the people of Kuppam with businesses, services, institutions, and opportunities. Our goal is to provide a single, trusted destination where residents, visitors, students, and business owners can easily discover everything Kuppam has to offer.

Whether you're searching for a restaurant, hospital, school, property, transport service, or local business, Hello Kuppam helps you find accurate and up-to-date information quickly and conveniently. Finding reliable local information can often be time-consuming — Hello Kuppam brings it all together in one user-friendly platform, connecting the local community while giving businesses and organizations a digital presence.

Mission

To build a trusted digital platform that connects people with verified local information, promotes local businesses, supports community growth, and makes discovering services in Kuppam simple, fast, and reliable.

Vision

To become the most trusted digital information platform for Kuppam, helping residents, visitors, and businesses stay connected while supporting the town's digital growth and development.

How Hello Kuppam Works

1. Sign In

Sign in using your Google account to get started.

2. Explore or List

Browse local businesses and services, or request permission to publish your own listings.

3. Review & Approval

Content submitted by providers is reviewed by the Super Admin before publication.

4. Goes Live

Once approved, listings become publicly visible to everyone.

5. Engage

Search, view, rate, review, comment on, and share listings with others.
